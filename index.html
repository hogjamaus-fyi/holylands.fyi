Framework

The New Jerusalem Framework

New Jerusalem (HL) is framed as a living proof of God's presence. In this blueprint, reconciliation is not grounded in fragile political compromise, but in the miracles of God expressed through direct acts of faith and a visible, practical economy of abundance.

Under the HL nation code, the land is described as expanding into a green and restorative home where divine favor produces ecological renewal, material stability, and a compelling alternative to conflict rooted in scarcity.

Chaldean / Christian Anchor

Supernatural Healing, Fellowship, and Spiritual Hospitality.

Dual Pillars

Direct faith and practical economics operating together

01

Direct Miracles for Faith

Sanctuaries, prayer towers, and healing centers create space for spiritual breakthroughs, physical healing, and profound restoration, keeping people oriented toward awe and relationship with the Creator.

02

Practical Economic Value from God

Unexpected springs, exceptional harvests, and a friction-light economy are presented as material signs of favor, reducing desperation and replacing historical scarcity with durable prosperity.

Spatial Harmony

Autonomous choice with unified prosperity

Separate Autonomous Zones

The blueprint keeps living quarters, schools, and primary places of worship distinct for HL-CE, HL-PS, and HL-IL, allowing each community to maintain its traditions and internal rhythms without imposed compromise.

The THL Shared Hubs

The Holy Land centers act as cooperative spaces where agricultural surpluses, recovery services, and shared resources are coordinated so each community benefits from pooled abundance while remaining locally grounded.
