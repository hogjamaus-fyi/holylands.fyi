6-Step Action Plan Around a Star of David

This action plan expands the New Jerusalem framework into practical, faith-led steps for alignment, authority, prayer, and rebuilding.

Action Plan

Choose God, Clarify Authority, Rebuild with Purpose

Step 1

Choose God in Full, Now

Commit fully and immediately to God so every next choice starts from devotion rather than fear.

Step 2

Take Commands From Israel

Anchor direction in Israel itself, not far-away voices with no covenant responsibility on the ground.

Step 3

Ask God for Help with Anything

Bring every burden, gap, and decision to God, expecting practical help and clear guidance.

Step 4

Pray Like Lives Depend on It

Pray urgently and consistently for anything confusing, dangerous, or unfinished until peace and direction arrive.

Step 5

Affirm Israel as Its Own Authority

Communicate with clarity and confidence from self-governance, recognizing Israel as its own master and authority in all decisions.

Step 6

Ask God for Protection and Begin Rebuild

Ask God to confront your true enemies with justice, protect your people, and launch a disciplined rebuild.
